About On-Time Logistics Company

Who We Are

On-Time Logistics Company operates a fleet of 30 refrigerated trucks, providing fast, secure, and temperature-controlled cargo transportation between Europe, Georgia, and Central Asia — in both directions.

What We Do

Refrigerated Transport

Purpose-built reefer trucks hold temperature-sensitive goods within range from pickup to delivery.

Full Container Load (FCL)

Full container shipments in every standard and special configuration — including refrigerated, open top, flat rack, and tank containers.

Less than Container Load (LCL)

Weekly cargo consolidation and warehousing from anywhere in the world into the Caucasus, with departures from Hong Kong, Shanghai, Qingdao, and the US.

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated full-truckload transport with a single truck per shipment — direct routes from Europe, China, and across Georgia.

Less than Truckload (LTL)

Consolidated groupage shipments from Europe, China, and within Georgia — sharing truck space to keep costs down.

Container Transport from Poti & Batumi

Full container delivery from Georgia's Black Sea ports, on to destinations across Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan.

Export Services

Full export support by sea and by land — moving cargo out of Georgia through ports or border crossings.

Coverage

One lane, both directions

Our reefer fleet runs the same corridor both ways — no empty legs, no delays waiting for a return load.
